# RSS Reader

Follow your RSS feeds and read new posts in one list.

Add the address of an RSS or Atom feed and its recent posts appear in your
Unread list, newest first, under Today, Yesterday and then dates. Tap a post
to see a short excerpt, open the full article in your browser, save it for
later, or mark it unread again. A Save for later tab keeps what you want to
come back to, and All shows everything including posts you have read.

Feeds, read state and saved posts are private to each person. Feeds are
checked for new posts when the list is opened; a feed that fails to update
keeps its older posts and is marked in the feeds list.

The staging preview opened with `?demo=1` shows a set of made-up feeds and
posts, labelled "Staging demo: sample feeds and posts".
